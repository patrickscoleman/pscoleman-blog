<section class="poem">
<p lang="ja" class="original-text" hidden>古池や 蛙飛びこむ 水の音</p>
<p>An old pond and there<br />
a frog is jumping into<br />
the sound of water</p>
</section>

<section class="poem">
<p lang="ja" class="original-text" hidden>夏草や 兵どもが 夢の跡</p>
<p>Summer grasses and<br />
batallions of soldiers are<br />
but the scars of dreams</p>
</section>

<section class="poem">
<p lang="ja" class="original-text" hidden>草の戸も 住み替る代ぞ 雛の家</p>
<p>I left my thatched hut<br />
and moved back into the world!<br />
It&#x27;s now home to dolls</p>
</section>

<section class="poem">
<p lang="ja" class="original-text" hidden>降る音や 耳も酢うなる 梅の雨</p>
<p>The sound of rainfall<br />
It falls upon my ears and<br />
the pickled ume</p>
</section>

<section class="poem">
<p lang="ja" class="original-text" hidden>蜘何と 音をなにと鳴く 秋の風</p>
<p>Spider, how do you<br />
sound and what is it you cry<br />
into the fall wind?</p>
</section>

<section class="poem">
<p lang="ja" class="original-text" hidden>寂しさや 岩にしみ込む 蝉の聲</p>
<p>Loneliness and the<br />
rocks even are soaked through by<br />
the cicadas&#x27; chirps</p>
</section>

<section class="poem">
<p lang="ja" class="original-text" hidden>朝茶飲む 僧静かなり 菊の花</p>
<p>Drinking morning tea<br />
the monks grow silent among<br />
the chrysanthemums</p>
</section>

<section class="poem">
<p lang="ja" class="original-text" hidden>富士の山 蚤が茶臼の 覆ひかな</p>
<p>At Mt. Fuji and<br />
fleas are on the tea grinder<br />
completely covered</p>
</section>

<section class="poem">
<p lang="ja" class="original-text" hidden>旅に病んで 夢は枯野を かけ迴る</p>
<p>Sick on my travels<br />
over dead meadows, my dreams<br />
gallop, circling</p>
</section>

<section class="poem">
<p lang="ja" class="original-text" hidden>曙や 霧にうづまく 鐘の声</p>
<p>At daybreak and the<br />
fog sends things whirling about<br />
a bell is sounding</p>
</section>
